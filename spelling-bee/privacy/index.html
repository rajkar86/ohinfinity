Spelling Bee Unlimited: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Short version: the app does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, stats and settings (theme, board size, word list choice, saved puzzle) are stored on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • The app does not connect to the internet while you play.

Feedback by email

Settings has a Give feedback option. It opens a draft message in your own email app, addressed to kraj.console+spellingbee@gmail.com. The draft includes the app version, your device model, your operating system version, your board size, your theme and your language setting, to help us understand a problem. You can read and edit the message. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you send it, we receive your email address and what is in the message, and we use it only to read and reply to you. We do not share it with anyone.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
